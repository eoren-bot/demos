Webexpenses

Automates invoice processing and supplier chasing, freeing the AP team for higher-value judgement calls.

UK employer — Webexpenses (role: Accounts Payable Assistant).

Covered: 95% (agent 71% + your approvals 14% + our assistant 10%)

Not covered (1): Shadow the Assistant Management Accountant during month-end preparation

What this would save: likely a few hours a week — rough estimate only; actual savings depend on volume. Every draft stays with you to approve before anything leaves.

Systems: Webexpenses, Zudello, Excel
