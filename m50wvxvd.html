Carnegie Group

Automates ordering, scheduling and document tasks so your Construction Administrator focuses on projects.

Steel-frame design-and-build contractor for commercial and industrial buildings — end-to-end concept-to-completion across the South East. Scope is industrial units, warehouses, workshops, and commercial steel-frame buildings. No agricultural sheds, no residential housing.

Covered: 93% (agent 80% + your approvals 7% + our assistant 7%) · voice add-on 7%

What this would save: likely 2–3 hours a week — rough upper-bound only; actual savings depend on your workflow and CRM. Method: ~5 minutes to review each pre-drafted CRM record / reply / booking email vs ~15–25 minutes end-to-end (log the enquiry, chase missing details, pick a diary slot, draft a reply or supplier booking), across roughly 6–10 inbound items a week.
