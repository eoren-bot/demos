Carnegie Group

Frees up Carnegie Group's Construction Administrator to focus on live project support and client relationships by handling the repetitive document, scheduling, and filing tasks that come with a busy 12-month fixed-term role across Contracts, Site, and Estimating teams.

Steel-frame design-and-build construction company based in Crawley — end-to-end concept-to-completion service across the South East. Employee-owned; group includes Carnegie Steel Buildings, Carnegie Architecture, and BM Steels.

Honest coverage: Covers 1 of 9 duties fully, 6 partly.

What this would save: likely 2–3 hours a week — rough upper-bound only; actual savings depend on your workflow and CRM. Method: ~5 minutes to review each pre-drafted CRM record / reply / booking email vs ~15–25 minutes end-to-end (log the enquiry, chase missing details, pick a diary slot, draft a reply or supplier booking), across roughly 6–10 inbound items a week.
