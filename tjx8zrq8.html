#5 Duty 4: Answer customer questions partial

Input sample

Pasted inbox message — received Mon 9 Nov 2026, 08:14 From: Alice Underhill Subject: Do I need a structural engineer for my loft conversion? Hi there, I am planning to convert my loft into a bedroom and was wondering whether I actually need a structural engineer involved, or whether my builder can just get on with it. My builder says he has done loads of these before and can sort it himself. I have also heard something about Building Regulations — do I need to worry about that side of things too? I want to make sure I am doing this properly but I do not want to pay for things I do not need. Thanks, Alice Underhill FAQ / service notes to draw on: - Loft conversions almost always require structural calculations for the new floor joists, any new steel beams, and changes to the existing roof structure. - Building Control (Building Regulations) will require stamped structural calculations from a chartered structural engineer before they sign off the work. - StructSpectrum Ltd provides a full loft conversion calcs pack covering floor, roof, and any steelwork. - Typical turnaround once instructed is 5 to 10 working days.

Agent output

DRAFT RESPONSE — for StructSpectrum Ltd to review and send To: Alice Underhill Subject: Re: Do I need a structural engineer for my loft conversion? Dear Alice, Thank you for getting in touch. This is a really common question, so we are happy to clear it up. In almost all loft conversions, a structural engineer is required. Even an experienced builder cannot self-certify the structural design — Building Control (Building Regulations) will expect to see stamped structural calculations prepared by a chartered structural engineer before they will sign off the work. Without these, your builder could be asked to stop, or the work may not pass inspection. The calculations we typically provide for a loft conversion cover the new floor joists, any steel beams needed to support the altered roof, and changes to the existing roof structure. This gives you and your builder a clear, approved set of figures to build from. Once we receive your instructions and the relevant drawings or measurements, our usual turnaround is 5 to 10 working days. If you would like to proceed or have further questions, please do reply and we will be glad to help. Kind regards, StructSpectrum Ltd AGENT NOTE — back-and-forth needed: if Alice asks about site visits or whether her specific roof type affects scope, please review before responding.
