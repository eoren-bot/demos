StructSpectrum Ltd

Automates enquiry replies, quotes, and follow-ups so the coordinator wins more bookings faster.

UK structural-engineering consultancy. Calculations, design and reports for homeowners, builders, architects and surveyors — fully remote, growing fast, small-business scale.

Covered: 100% (agent 89% + our assistant 11%)

What this would save: likely 4–6 hours a week — rough upper-bound only; actual savings depend on enquiry volume. Method: ~5 minutes to review each pre-drafted quote / follow-up / job record vs ~10–20 minutes end-to-end to triage an enquiry, draft a quote, chase missing details, send a follow-up, and update the job record, across roughly 20–40 enquiries a week.

Systems: AI tools, online systems
