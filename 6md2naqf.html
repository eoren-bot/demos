Complete Fixing Solutions

Frees up time for accuracy-critical admin by automating timesheets, POs, and invoice reconciliation at Complete Fixing Solutions.

High-end joinery and metalwork fit-out specialist, roughly 34 staff — bespoke installation and fit-out packages for manufacturers, fabricators, and principal contractors. Scope is interior fit-out joinery and metalwork only; no agricultural, residential, or large civils work.

Honest coverage: Covers 3 of 11 duties fully, 5 partly.

What this would save: likely 3–5 hours a week — rough upper-bound only; actual savings depend on your week's paperwork volume. Method: ~5 minutes to review each pre-drafted record / draft / flag vs ~15–25 minutes end-to-end to key timesheets into Sage, chase missing PO refs, reconcile invoices against delivery notes, and draft the paperwork, across roughly 15–30 inbound items a week.

Systems: Sage
