#3 Duty 3: Reconcile bank statements and company accounts on a weekly basis agent

Agent pulls the Xero bank feed, matches transactions to posted entries, and flags unreconciled items for review.

Input sample

Automated trigger: Monday morning reconciliation run — Mon 9 Nov 2026 Bank feed period: Fri 6 Nov 2026 Xero bank feed pulled for WeBuyVintage current account. Feed summary from Fri 6 Nov 2026: Total transactions in feed: 41 Auto-matched to posted entries: 39 Unmatched transactions: 2 Unmatched item 1: Debit £342.80 — reference INV-A101, dated Mon 2 Nov 2026, no PO reference found in Xero Unmatched item 2: Debit £1,610.00 — reference INV-A103, dated Wed 4 Nov 2026, expected bill value £1,450.00 Please run reconciliation, log matched items in Xero, and email the bookkeeper an exceptions list for sign-off.

Agent output

RECONCILIATION RUN — Mon 9 Nov 2026 (bank feed date: Fri 6 Nov 2026) AUTO-MATCHED AND RECONCILED IN XERO 39 of 41 transactions matched to posted Xero entries and marked as reconciled. No action required. EXCEPTIONS FLAGGED FOR BOOKKEEPER REVIEW — 2 items Item 1: INV-A101 — Debit £342.80 (Mon 2 Nov 2026) Issue: No PO reference on invoice or in Xero. Cannot confirm purchase authorisation. Action required: Your bookkeeper to locate PO or confirm approval before reconciling. Item 2: INV-A103 — Debit £1,610.00 (Wed 4 Nov 2026) Issue: Invoice total £1,610.00 exceeds expected amount of £1,450.00. Variance of £160.00 unaccounted for. #5 Duty 5: Monitor cash flow and report any discrepancies agent

Automated Xero cash-position pull completed for Mon 9 Nov 2026. Today's closing bank balance retrieved from Xero: bank balance on file (masked for report). Rolling weekly average balance calculated from Mon 2 Nov 2026 to Fri 6 Nov 2026 (last five trading days). Variance alert threshold set at: more than ten percent below the rolling weekly average. Today's closing balance is below the rolling weekly average by a material amount — threshold breached.
