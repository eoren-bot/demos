Agent posts sales invoices and supplier bills to Xero, maintains ledger records, and flags anomalies for review.

Input sample

Monthly purchase ledger batch — November 2026 processing run. Please review the following three supplier invoices received this week and match against open POs in Xero. Supplier: Brightwell Marine Supplies (contact: Jack Brightwell, j.brightwell@brightwellmarine.example.co.uk) Invoice INV-A101, dated Mon 2 Nov 2026, amount £342.80. No PO reference quoted on the invoice document. Supplier: Ellsworth Subsea Equipment Ltd (contact: Ivy Ellsworth, i.ellsworth@ellsworthsubsea.example.co.uk) Invoice INV-A102, dated Tue 3 Nov 2026, amount £218.40. Delivery note not yet received at our yard. Supplier: Wyndham Offshore Services (contact: Emma Wyndham, e.wyndham@wyndhamos.example.co.uk) Invoice INV-A103, dated Wed 4 Nov 2026, amount £1,610.00. Corresponding PO expected value £1,450.00. Combined total across all three invoices: £2,171.20. Please post confirmed bills to Xero and flag any anomalies before the November payment run.
