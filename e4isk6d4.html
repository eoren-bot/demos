#1 Duty 1: Processing supplier invoices and payments. agent

Agent reads incoming supplier invoices from the shared inbox, posts bills to Xero, and prepares a payment-run proposal for client release.

Input sample

From: n.oakenshaw@thornburymedical.example.co.uk To: the AP inbox Date: Wed 4 Nov 2026 Subject: Invoice INV-A103 — November Care Consumables Supply Hi, Please find attached our invoice INV-A103 dated Wed 4 Nov 2026 for the supply of incontinence pads, disposable gloves, and hygiene products to London Paramount Care residential units. Invoice total: £1,610.00 Our PO reference: LPC-PO-0047 Payment terms: 30 days from invoice date Please process at your earliest convenience. Bank details on file. Regards, Niamh Oakenshaw Accounts, Thornbury Medical Supplies

Agent output

INVOICE PROCESSING SUMMARY — Mon 9 Nov 2026 London Paramount Care — Accounts Payable BILL POSTED Supplier: Thornbury Medical Supplies Invoice ref: INV-A103 | Invoice date: Wed 4 Nov 2026 Due date: Fri 4 Dec 2026 (30-day terms) Invoice total: £1,610.00 Posted to Xero as draft bill INV-A103, coded to Care Consumables expense account. PO MATCH RESULT — VARIANCE FLAGGED Matched to PO ref LPC-PO-0047. PO expected amount: £1,450.00 Invoice amount: £1,610.00 Variance: £160.00 above expected. Bill held in Xero as draft pending your finance manager's approval before authorisation. PAYMENT RUN PROPOSAL Proposed BACS payment of £1,450.00 (PO-agreed amount) held pending variance resolution. Full payment of £1,610.00 can be released once your accounts team confirms the overage is approved. ACTION REQUIRED Please review the £160.00 variance on INV-A103 and confirm whether to approve the full £1,610.00 or request a credit note from Thornbury Medical Supplies. Payment proposal queued for approval in the Outlook approval queue.

#3 Duty 4: Completing bank reconciliations and checking expenses. agent

Agent runs the Xero bank reconciliation daily, matches transactions to posted bills and receipts, and flags unmatched items for review.

Input sample

From: our shared accounts mailbox Received: Mon 9 Nov 2026, 08:04 Please run the Xero bank reconciliation for Friday 6 Nov 2026. The bank feed for that day has loaded overnight. We expect most items to clear against posted bills and carer expense receipts. Flag anything that cannot be auto-matched so the finance manager can review before the morning stand-up. Bank feed date: Fri 6 Nov 2026 Feed items loaded: 45 Posted bills and receipts available in Xero: current as of end of day Fri 6 Nov 2026 Outstanding supplier invoices logged last week for context: INV-A101 Mon 2 Nov 2026 £342.80 — no PO reference noted INV-A102 Tue 3 Nov 2026 £218.40 — delivery note not yet received INV-A103 Wed 4 Nov 2026 £1,610.00 — queried; expected amount was £1,450.00 (variance £160.00) Please summarise results and queue a review email for the finance manager.

Agent output

RECONCILIATION RUN — Fri 6 Nov 2026 bank feed Processed by agent: Mon 9 Nov 2026 System: Xero RESULTS Feed items loaded: 45 Auto-matched: 42 Unmatched (held): 3 AUTO-MATCH SUMMARY 42 transactions matched to posted bills and carer expense receipts in Xero. No action required on these items. UNMATCHED ITEMS REQUIRING REVIEW 1. INV-A101 dated Mon 2 Nov 2026 £342.80 Reason: No PO reference on invoice — Xero cannot confirm purchase authorisation. Awaiting your finance manager to supply or waive the PO reference before the payment line can be matched. 2. INV-A102 dated Tue 3 Nov 2026 £218.40 Reason: Delivery note not received. Xero match held pending goods-received confirmation from your operations team. 3. INV-A103 dated Wed 4 Nov 2026 £1,610.00 Reason: Invoice total exceeds expected amount. Expected £1,450.00; variance is £160.00. Held in Xero pending supplier credit note or approval to pay full amount. TOTALS FOR AWARENESS Total invoiced across three held items: £2,171.20 Total if INV-A103 corrected to expected: £2,011.20 NEXT STEP Review email queued for approval in the Outlook approval queue — addressed to your finance manager, summarising the three unmatched items and requesting sign-off decisions before close of business Mon 9 Nov 2026.
#4 Duty 5: Investigating payment discrepancies and following up outstanding invoices. agent

Agent identifies discrepancies from Xero reports, drafts query or chaser emails to suppliers/customers via the approval queue, and logs outcomes.

Customer invoice outstanding: Invoice ref: LPC-OUT-0847 Customer: Harrow Council Adult Social Care commissioning team Contact on file: Nathan Merriweather (commissioning manager) Email on file: n.merriweather@harrowcouncil.example.co.uk Invoice date: Mon 2 Nov 2026 Due date: Thu 5 Nov 2026 (30-day net from issue, early payment terms applied) Amount outstanding: £1,610.00 Status in Xero: Awaiting payment — now 4 days overdue Please draft a polite chaser email to Harrow Council, queue it for approval, and schedule a follow-up reminder if no response by Thu 12 Nov 2026.

DISCREPANCY CHECK — Mon 9 Nov 2026 Invoice LPC-OUT-0847 confirmed in Xero as awaiting payment. Amount £1,610.00. Due date was Thu 5 Nov 2026. Now 4 days overdue. No part-payment or remittance recorded in Xero against this invoice. ACTION 1 — CHASER EMAIL DRAFTED To: n.merriweather@harrowcouncil.example.co.uk Subject: Overdue Invoice LPC-OUT-0847 — London Paramount Care Dear Nathan, I hope this message finds you well. Could you please confirm receipt and advise on the expected payment date? If you require a copy of the invoice or have any queries, please do not hesitate to contact our accounts team. Kind regards, London Paramount Care Queued for approval in the Outlook approval queue — awaiting sign-off from your finance manager before sending. ACTION 2 — XERO NOTE LOGGED Activity note posted to Xero against LPC-OUT-0847: Chaser email drafted Mon 9 Nov 2026. Queued for approval. Awaiting payment of £1,610.00. #6 Duty 7: Supporting month-end procedures and general finance administration. agent

Expanded into concrete sub-tasks; majority are handled by the agent end-to-end.

Input sample

Today is Mon 30 Nov 2026. Please run the month-end close for October 2026 in Xero. Steps required: 1. Pull the aged-debt report for all outstanding client invoices as at 31 Oct 2026. 2. Compile a summary of all journals posted in October 2026 (accruals, prepayments, payroll journals). 3. Bundle the aged-debt report and journal summary into a single month-end pack and email it to the finance controller for review. 4. Once the controller confirms approval, lock the October 2026 period in Xero so no further postings can be made. Approval contact: your finance controller (internal — do not email an external address). Note: No sterling figures to be included; descriptive summaries only.

Agent output

MONTH-END CLOSE — OCTOBER 2026 Run date: Mon 30 Nov 2026 System: Xero STEP 1 — AGED-DEBT REPORT Pulled aged-debt report from Xero as at 31 Oct 2026. Report lists all outstanding client invoices by debtor, age bracket (current, 30, 60, 90+ days), and status. No sterling figures included per instruction. Report exported to Excel and saved as October-2026-Aged-Debt.xlsx. STEP 2 — JOURNAL SUMMARY Compiled October 2026 journal log from Xero covering: accruals journals (care staffing costs), prepayment release journals (insurance and software licences), and payroll journals. Summary exported to Excel as October-2026-Journal-Summary.xlsx. STEP 3 — MONTH-END PACK EMAILED Both files attached to a single email addressed to the finance controller (the shared accounts mailbox). Queued for approval in the Outlook approval queue before sending. Subject line: London Paramount Care — October 2026 Month-End Pack for Review. STEP 4 — PERIOD LOCK (PENDING CONTROLLER APPROVAL) October 2026 period in Xero is NOT yet locked. Agent is monitoring the approval queue. Once the finance controller confirms approval, the agent will lock the October 2026 financial period in Xero automatically and log the lock timestamp. No further action required from your accounts team until confirmation is received.

Agent keeps supplier and customer contact records current in Xero, updates payment terms, and flags stale or duplicate accounts for review.

Input sample

Our new bank details are enclosed in the attached secure PDF. Our supplier reference in your system is SUPP-0084. Could you please update your records at your earliest convenience and confirm receipt? Our payment terms remain 30 days net. Regards, Sebastian Whitlock Accounts, Hartwell Care Supplies Ltd

Agent output

ACTION LOG — Mon 9 Nov 2026 — London Paramount Care SUPPLIER RECORD UPDATE Supplier: Hartwell Care Supplies Ltd Xero supplier reference: SUPP-0084 Action: Bank details updated in Xero supplier contact record. New sort code and account number on file (masked). Payment terms confirmed as 30 days net — no change required. Updated in Xero: Mon 9 Nov 2026. CHANGE FLAG — CONTROLLER REVIEW REQUIRED A banking detail change was received by email from s.whitlock@hartwell-supplies.example.co.uk on Mon 9 Nov 2026 for supplier SUPP-0084 (Hartwell Care Supplies Ltd). This is a sensitive update. Your finance manager should verify the change directly with the supplier before any payment is released against the new details. CONFIRMATION EMAIL — QUEUED FOR APPROVAL To: s.whitlock@hartwell-supplies.example.co.uk Subject: Re: Updated banking details — Hartwell Care Supplies Ltd Body: Thank you for letting us know. We have updated your banking details in our records with effect from Mon 9 Nov 2026 and can confirm payment terms remain 30 days net. Agent reads queries from the shared inbox, pulls the relevant Xero record, drafts a response, and queues it for release via the M365 approval queue.

From: f.wyndham@northwingresidence.example.co.uk To: the AP inbox Date: Mon 9 Nov 2026, 09:14 Subject: Petty Cash Reimbursement - Still Not Received Hi, I submitted a petty cash reimbursement request last Tuesday (Tue 3 Nov 2026) for care-home supplies purchased at short notice. I was told to expect payment within a few working days but nothing has appeared yet. Could someone please check the status and let me know when I can expect this to come through? The delay is causing difficulty for the home's day-to-day supply budget. Many thanks, Florence Wyndham Manager, North Wing Residence

Agent output

STEP 1 — QUERY IDENTIFICATION Sender: Florence Wyndham (f.wyndham@northwingresidence.example.co.uk) Query type: Petty cash reimbursement status Submission date cited: Tue 3 Nov 2026 STEP 2 — XERO RECORD CHECK Searched Xero (London Paramount Care account) for a petty cash reimbursement linked to North Wing Residence submitted on Tue 3 Nov 2026. Result: Reimbursement claim found — recorded as a draft spend money transaction dated Tue 3 Nov 2026, currently awaiting internal authorisation. No payment has been issued yet. STEP 3 — EMAIL DRAFT To: f.wyndham@northwingresidence.example.co.uk Subject: Re: Petty Cash Reimbursement - Still Not Received Dear Florence, Thank you for getting in touch.
