Haddenham Healthcare

Automates supplier invoice processing and payment runs, freeing time for supplier relationships.

UK healthcare manufacturer — Haddenham Healthcare makes specialist compression garments and wound-care products. Supplier base includes specialist fabrics, medical components, and packaging vendors.

Covered: 100% (agent 72% + your approvals 17% + our assistant 11%)

What this would save: likely 4–7 hours a week — rough upper-bound only; actual savings depend on supplier volume. Method: ~5 minutes to review each pre-drafted reconciliation / payment-run entry / supplier query vs ~10–20 minutes end-to-end to match an invoice to the PO and delivery note, flag variances, chase missing paperwork, and prepare the month-end position, across roughly 30–60 invoices a week.

Systems: Prophet21, Sage, QuickBooks, Microsoft Excel Accounting: other — integration: hard
