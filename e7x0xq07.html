A person at the company checks every output before anything is sent, filed, or paid. The agent drafts; a human signs off.

Automates invoice processing, supplier reconciliation, and payment-run prep to free up time for supplier relationships.

UK healthcare manufacturer — Haddenham Healthcare makes specialist compression garments and wound-care products. Supplier base includes specialist fabrics, medical components, and packaging vendors.

Covered: 100% (agent 61% + your approvals 22% + our assistant 17%)

What this would save: likely 4–7 hours a week — rough upper-bound only; actual savings depend on supplier volume. Method: ~5 minutes to review each pre-drafted reconciliation / payment-run entry / supplier query vs ~10–20 minutes end-to-end to match an invoice to the PO and delivery note, flag variances, chase missing paperwork, and prepare the month-end position, across roughly 30–60 invoices a week.

Systems: Microsoft Excel, Prophet21, Sage, QuickBooks Accounting: other — integration: hard

#3 Duty 3: Resolve any discrepancies between invoices and purchase orders. agent

Agent flags discrepancies, drafts a query email to the supplier via the approval queue, and logs the dispute in the accounting system.

No credit note or change order on file to account for the difference. Payment terms: 30 days from invoice date (due Fri 4 Dec 2026)

Agent output

DISCREPANCY DETECTED Invoice INV-A103 from Whitlock Medical Textiles (Wed 4 Nov 2026) totals £1,610.00 against approved PO-7741 value of £1,450.00. Variance is £160.00. No change order or credit note found to explain the difference. GRN-7741 confirms all 200 units were received, so the quantity is not in dispute — the unit pricing or an additional charge appears to be the source of the overcharge. ACTION — BILL HELD IN SAGE INV-A103 posted to Sage as a draft bill in dispute status, flagged with variance note of £160.00. Bill will not be released to payment run until discrepancy is resolved.
